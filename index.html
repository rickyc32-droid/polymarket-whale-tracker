Methodology

This tracker is a research experiment, not an NFL EDGE input. It begins with a current Polymarket Sports top-30 cohort, reads each public wallet's open positions, and looks for same-outcome overlap in the selected league.

Consensus weight

Wallets are weighted transparently by leaderboard rank and relative P&L inside the current cohort. This is a consensus score—not a claim that a wallet's true predictive probability is known.

Copyability filter

The tracker compares the whales' weighted average entry with the price available now. Strong agreement can still be labeled PASS — PRICE MOVED when followers would be chasing the move.

Important limitations

  • Top-30 selection creates survivorship/leaderboard bias.
  • Unique wallets are not guaranteed to be independent information sources.
  • Public APIs, positions and order books can lag or be incomplete.
  • Spread, depth and fees can erase apparent edge.
  • Paper results need a large out-of-sample history before drawing conclusions.

No wallet key, private key, seed phrase or trading credential is requested or stored.
